# Code of conduct

Project IGI Studio follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, and keep criticism about
the work, never the person. Harassment, insults, and personal or political
attacks have no place here, in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere
else the project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, tell the maintainer, Nouman Ahsan
([@NaumanHSA](https://github.com/NaumanHSA)), privately: on this repository's
**Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and say it is about
conduct. Reports are read in confidence, and the maintainer may remove
comments, close threads or block anyone who does not stop, as the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe.
